# What's New in Real-Time Physics and Graphics (mid-2024 to Sept 2026): Research for bobo

*Compiled 2026-09-29. This extends `docs/research/physics.md`, `docs/research/cloth-knitting.md` and `docs/research/shear-knit.md`. It skips things those docs already cover: VBD, AVBD, OGC, JGS2, the cubic barrier / ppf-contact-solver, Granule-in-Cell, Genesis, Newton basics, box2d3-wasm, jure/webphysics, holtsetio/flow, matsuoka WebGPU-Ocean/WaterBall, jeantimex, and the Safari 26 WebGPU launch. Where one of those has news, it appears only as an update.*

**Legend.** **(est)** means my own estimate of phone performance (iPhone 13 mini, A15, 60 fps, in a browser or WKWebView), not a measured number. **[unverified]** means I could not open the source, or the claim comes only from a secondary summary. Every link in this doc was opened (HTTP 200 or read) unless it is marked † (taken from the Ke-Sen Huang paper index and not opened individually) or [unverified].

---

## 1. Summary

1. **Mixwell (SIGGRAPH 2026 Best Paper, Doug and Ethan James)** is the biggest find for bobo. It gives analytic "fluid brushes" for paper marbling (combs, tines, Nonpareil and Gel-Git patterns). They are evaluated per pixel in a shader, with no grid solve. There is an **MIT-licensed GLSL library**, a WebGPU demo and Shadertoys. This is a near-free **Marbling Tray** toy, and a new comb tool for Ink Swirl's Paint mode.
2. **Dripping Thin Films for Real-time Digital Painting (Eurographics 2026, Adobe)** is a grid thin-film height field that carries pigment and makes controllable drip "fingers". There is a **WebGL2 web demo**. It fits a **Drip Wall / Paint Pour** toy, or a "tilt to drip" mode for Ink Swirl.
3. **Fire-X (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025; Two Minute Papers, Feb 2026)** tracks fuel, oxygen, CO₂ and water vapour with stoichiometric heat release. That gives blue-to-orange flames, fires that starve for air, and water suppression. Campfire can take the *ideas* cheaply: two extra scalar fields on its existing 2D grid **(est)**.
4. **Real-time vortex-preserving smoke is now a research focus.** Examples are Leapfrog Flow Maps (SIGGRAPH 2025, titled "…for Real-Time Fluid Simulation", CUDA code), Vortex Particle Flow Maps (SIGGRAPH 2025, code) and Cirrus. A 2D port would give Ink Swirl and Campfire long-lived swirls and a **Smoke Ring cannon** toy **(est)**.
5. **Kinetic (lattice-Boltzmann) free-surface flows and foams (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025, code with a 2D foam demo)** simulate bubbles that stick, drain and pop. LBM maps very well to WebGPU, so a 2D **Soap Suds / Beer Foam** toy is realistic **(est)**.
6. **Stable Cosserat Rods (SIGGRAPH 2025; Two Minute Papers, Jan 2026, "45x faster")** is a robust, cheap rod solver with GPU code (YarnBall). It is also now in **Jolt 5.6 (July 2026) as GPU Cosserat hair**. It is the right core for the roadmap's *Rope & yarn ball* and *Knit-a-Scarf*. **Real-Time Knit Deformation and Rendering (SIGGRAPH 2025)** reports 1 ms per frame of yarn simulation on an RTX 3090 and upgrades *Pull the Thread*'s look.
7. **Browser WebGPU physics demos matured.** matsuoka-601's **Particles4All** (Aug 2026) runs a unified PBD solver in WebGPU where fluid and rigid bodies share one constraint loop (buoyancy for free), with an anisotropic-kernel surface and surface tension. **Floaty** (2D PBF plus soft bodies, WASM threads) and holtsetio's **softbodies / breeze / aurelia** (three.js TSL) are also out. **three.js now ships** `webgpu_compute_particles_fluid` (MLS-MPM, May 2025), `webgpu_compute_cloth` (May 2025) and `webgpu_volume_fire` (June 2026).
8. **Engines.** Box2D v3.1 (Apr 2025) added rolling resistance, tangent speed (conveyor belts), sensors and a character mover. Erin Catto **announced Box3D** (June 2026, alpha, deterministic, with replay). Jolt went to 5.6 with a GPU compute interface and Cosserat hair. **JoltPhysics.js reached 1.0** (Dec 2025; 1.1 in July 2026). Newton went 1.0 (Apr 2026) to 1.6 (Sep 2026) and now has `implicit_mpm`, `vbd`, `style3d` and `xpbd` solvers, but it needs CUDA, so it is a reference only.
9. **Fracture and breaking with real-time angles:** Stressful Tree Modeling (SIGGRAPH 2025, *interactive* branch breaking with strands and Cosserat rods), DeepFracture (EG 2026, learned brittle fracture patterns from impulse, with a live Hugging Face demo), an Incompressible Crack Model for MPM (I3D 2025), and Two-Pass Shock Propagation for stable Gauss-Seidel stacking (SCA 2025). These give a **Snap the Branch** toy, plus better Demolition Tower stacks and shatter patterns.
10. **Cheap "juice" papers:** constant-time Glinty NDFs (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025, with a Shadertoy) for glittering sand and snow; Physics-inspired procedural texturing of extremely deformable surfaces (SIGGRAPH 2026, code), whose textures wrinkle and re-scale instead of smearing on jelly and goo; Multi-dimensional Procedural Wave Noise (SIGGRAPH 2025, with an interactive demo); and Improving Curl Noise (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025) [content unverified].
11. **The iOS browser platform is stable rather than exploding.** WebGPU shipped in Safari 26.0. Safari 26.1 to 26.6 were mostly fixes. **Safari 27.0 (Sep 17, 2026)** adds WGSL `clip_distances` and WebAssembly JSPI. Many showcase WebGPU physics demos still say "Chrome only" (e.g. jure/webphysics), so every new toy still needs testing on a real iPhone.
12. **Gaussian-splat physics** (PhysGaussian and successors such as i-PhysGaussian, 2026, with implicit MPM at 20x larger time steps; VR-Doh, SIGGRAPH 2025, real-time MPM clay rendered with 3DGS) is still desktop-GPU work. The phone-feasible version is a **coarse cage or shape-matching deformer driving splats** rendered with **Spark 2.x** (World Labs; MIT; claims to be fast on low-power mobile; per-splat dynamic edits). That points to a "poke a real object" toy **(est)**.

## 3. Per-area notes

### 3.1 Liquids (grid, particle, hybrid)
- **The flow-map family** is where 2025 fluids research went. At SIGGRAPH 2025 it included VPFM, Clebsch gauge on particle flow maps, Cirrus, **Leapfrog Flow Maps**, compressible flow maps and EDGE. At SIGGRAPH Asia 2025 it added an adjoint differentiable flow-map method, and at SIGGRAPH 2026 DiffSurFlow. For bobo, the practical takeaway is that **carrying vorticity or impulse on particles keeps swirls alive**. Ink Swirl can get most of this with a 2D vortex-particle layer on top of Stable Fluids **(est)**.
- **IPBF** (Utah, SIGGRAPH Asia 2025) makes PBF-style water stable at extreme time steps with less compression. It is worth trying if Splash Tank ever goes particle-only or needs a CPU/WASM fallback.
- **Fast VEM Fluid Simulation** (SIGGRAPH 2026, Nankai)† and **Wavelet Fluids** (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025)† are new discretisations. Both are offline for now.
- **Unified Pressure, Surface Tension and Friction for SPH** (SIGGRAPH 2025)† and **Implicit Incompressible Porous Flow with SPH** (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025, SPlisHSPlasH fork)† are both useful for "sponge soaks up water" ideas.
- **Adaptive Optical Layers / tall cells** (EG 2026)† and **Quadtree Tall Cells** (SIGGRAPH 2025) are cheap deep-water ideas. Ripple Pool's height field already covers most of this ground.

### 3.2 Smoke, fire, explosions
- **Fire-X** is the headline. Borrowable pieces for Campfire:
  - (a) an **oxygen field** that combustion consumes, so covering the fire with a lid or smothering it with sand chokes it;
  - (b) a **fuel-type-dependent flame colour** (blue methane-like to orange sooty);
  - (c) **water droplets turning into steam** that cools the flame and displaces oxygen.
- **FlameForge** adds **char depth as an insulating layer** and structural collapse via PBD coupling. That maps onto Campfire's CPU burn cells (a char-depth scalar) and the logs falling apart.
- **Scintilla** (combustible vegetation for wildfires; TMP Nov 2024) is a reference for a "burning bonsai" variant.
- **three.js `webgpu_volume_fire`** (added June 2026) is a volumetric-lighting fire example in TSL, worth reading for a 3D fire look.
- **Real-Time 3D Simulation of Heat-Induced Air Turbulence** ([arXiv 2603.02048](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.02048)) [unverified: seen only in search results] covers heat shimmer, a cheap post-process over Campfire.

### 3.3 MPM, granular, snow, mud, clay
- **CK-MPM** swaps the quadratic B-spline for a compact C² kernel on a dual grid. It is faster and less diffusive, but it touches the kernel code in both Goo Lab and Splash Tank, so benchmark P2G atomics on the A15.
- **Incompressible crack MPM** (I3D 2025) gives a new "dough / bread" material with tearing strands.
- **VR-Doh's localised simulation**: only simulate particles near the finger at full rate, and let the rest sleep. That is the key perf trick for a sculpting toy.
- **Numerical Homogenization of Sand** and the earlier **Granule-in-Cell** (already in physics.md) point to better sand. Goo Lab can adopt their friction and cohesion parameter curves.
- **Digital Animation of Powder-Snow Avalanches** (SIGGRAPH 2025)† is inspiration for a snow-slope toy, done cheaply with CA or MPM.
- **Arenite** (SIGGRAPH 2025, sandstone erosion) is offline. The *input* model is still a good toy concept: paint erodable areas, then blow wind or run water over them.

### 3.4 Rods, hair, yarn, knots
- **Stable Cosserat Rods** gives rods stable at large dt and high stiffness. Jolt 5.6 now ships Cosserat hair on GPU compute, which confirms the method is production-ready. On the phone, a CPU TS/WASM version with 1K to 3K vertices fits the Rope & yarn ball toy **(est)**.
- **Real-Time Knit Deformation and Rendering** and **Neighbor-Aware Stitch Mesh Relaxation** (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025) could make *Pull the Thread*'s knit look yarn-level. **Surface Power Diagrams for Knit Singularity Placement** (SIGGRAPH 2026)† and **Curl Quantization for Knit Singularities** (SIGGRAPH 2025)† help if Knit-a-Scarf needs shaping.
- **Rest Shape Optimization for Sag-Free Discrete Elastic Rods** (EG 2025)† solves "my rope sags under its own weight on the first frame".
- **Hair Mesh Rendering** already runs as a WebGL2 page, so it is a direct lead for Shear's fleece.

### 3.5 Cloth and contact
Already covered in bobo's docs: OGC, JGS2, AVBD and the cubic barrier. New:
- **Domain-decomposed Projective Dynamics on CPU** (SIGGRAPH 2025; TMP Feb 2026). Relevant because Tear Sheet and Laundry Line run cloth on the CPU. Domain decomposition across Web Workers is an avenue if bobo enables COOP/COEP threads **(est)**.
- **Physics-inspired estimation of optimal cloth mesh resolution** (SIGGRAPH 2025; TMP Jan 2026).
- **Cloth Animation with Time-dependent Persistent Wrinkles** (EG 2025, code)†. Crumple memory for a tablecloth or a paper toy.
- **Corotational Hinge-based Thin Plates/Shells** (EG 2025, code)† and **Better Bending / Bending-Active Cosserat** (SIGGRAPH 2026) are bending models for paper, rubber and metal. Useful for an origami or paper-crumple toy.
- **Real-Time Triangle-SDF CCD** (SCA 2025)† gives robust fast collisions of cloth against SDF props.

### 3.6 Rigid bodies, fracture, cutting
- **Two-Pass Shock Propagation** (SCA 2025) is a direct upgrade for Demolition's and Jelly Stack's tall stacks.
- **Box2D 3.1**: rolling resistance, tangent speed (conveyor belts), sensor redesign, character mover, and 2x faster CCD. **Box3D** was announced in June 2026 (alpha, deterministic, replay). There is no WASM build yet [as of the announcement], but it would be the natural 3D Demolition backend later.
- **DeepFracture** gives impulse-conditioned shatter. The phone path is to pre-bake pieces per impulse location bin.
- **Putting Rigid Bodies to Rest** (SIGGRAPH 2025)† predicts the resting poses of dice and toys, which could drive a dice-tray toy.
- **Matthias Müller's `tet-cut`** repo (Aug 2026) is a three.js prototype of a 64-entry lookup table for cutting tets by edge masks. It is a hint of a Ten Minute Physics cutting episode and directly relevant to a "slice the jelly" toy.

### 3.7 Soft bodies and creatures
- **holtsetio/softbodies**: three.js TSL port of TetSim soft bodies with collisions, running live in the browser.
- **Elastic locomotion** (SIGGRAPH 2025) and **FreeMusco** (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025, code)† are learned soft or muscular locomotion. For bobo, fake it with sinusoidal rest-shape actuation on shape-matching clusters.
- **Force-Dual Modes** (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025)† and **Interpolated Adaptive Linear ROM** (EG 2026)† are cheap subspace jiggle for many objects.

### 3.8 Bubbles, foam, thin films
- **HOME-FREE LBM** (foams with sharp interfaces; 2D foam-draining code). Most promising for a foam toy.
- **Unified multi-scale immersed bubbles** (Weta FX; EG 2025; TMP Oct 2025). Offline, but the "small bubbles as particles, big as grid" split is the pattern to copy.
- **Dripping Thin Films** (paint) and **Viscous Vortex Dynamics on Surfaces** (swirls on a soap-bubble surface).
- **Controllable Complex Freezing Dynamics on Thin Films** (SIGGRAPH 2025)† gives frost spreading on a bubble. Pairs nicely with a Bubble Wand toy (blow a bubble, let it freeze).

### 3.9 Paint, pigment, viscous
- **Mixwell** covers marbling and combing.
- **Dripping Thin Films** covers drips.
- Ink Swirl already has Kubelka-Munk-style mixing from physics.md. Mixwell's own "PaintColorMaps" OSL example shows how they colour marbled patterns.

### 3.10 Differentiable, neural, Gaussian-splat physics
- **What runs in real time:** VR-Doh (MPM plus 3DGS, desktop VR), and Neural Robot Dynamics (NVIDIA; TMP Nov 2025) for robots.
- **What is new but offline:** i-PhysGaussian (implicit MPM), GauSmoke and LagrangianSplats (SIGGRAPH 2026, smoke and fluid reconstruction into Gaussians, with code for LagrangianSplats)†, and Gaussian Fluids (SIGGRAPH 2025, a grid-free solver, not real time).
- **The phone path (est):** render with **Spark** and deform with a coarse shape-matching lattice or a 2D-to-3D cage. Treat splats like skinned vertices. Do not simulate per-splat.

### 3.11 Plants and growth
- **Real-Time GPU Tree Generation** (HPG 2025).
- **Stressful Tree Modeling** (breaking).
- **Autoregressive Generation of Static and Growing Trees** (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025)†.
- **LeafFit** (EG 2026)†.
- A cheap phone toy would combine a space-colonisation tree with Cosserat or XPBD branches **(est)**.

### 3.12 Browser platform (iOS, WebGPU)
- **Safari 26.0** shipped WebGPU (already in bobo's docs).
- **26.1** fixed GPUQueue and three.js video-texture bugs.
- **26.2** (Dec 2025) brought WebGPU in WebXR on visionOS and resizable Wasm memory buffers.
- **26.4** fixed WebGPU PNG pixel formats and added OffscreenCanvas in Workers for screenshot contexts.
- **27.0** (Sep 17, 2026) added WGSL `clip_distances` and Wasm JSPI.
- I found no new subgroup or f16 announcements in these notes, so do not assume subgroups on iOS. Keep feature detection in place.
- WebGPU physics demos still often say "Chrome only" (jure/webphysics), so test every compute path on the iPhone.

## 7. New toy ideas enabled

Ranked by satisfaction × feasibility. All budgets are for an iPhone 13 mini at 60 fps **(est)**.

1. **Marbling Tray** ★★★. Drip coloured inks onto a size bath, then drag combs, rakes and a stylus to make Turkish ebru, Nonpareil and Gel-Git patterns. "Lay the paper" captures a print, and the page fills with your prints.
   - Tech: Mixwell GLSL brush library (MIT); bake strokes into a texture every N strokes.
   - Budget: under 4 ms GPU at native resolution **(est)**.
   - Effort: S–M.
2. **Drip Wall / Paint Pour** ★★. Fling paint at a canvas, tilt the phone and watch drips race down. Colours mix where they cross.
   - Tech: Dripping Thin Films (grid thin film plus pigments) on WebGL2.
   - Budget: a 256×512 grid, about 3 to 5 ms **(est)**.
3. **Smoke Rings** ★★. Tap a vortex cannon to fire rings, fire two to see them leapfrog, and smash them into obstacles.
   - Tech: 2D (axisymmetric look) vortex particles or leapfrog flow maps with smoke dye, drawn over Ink Swirl's pipeline.
   - Effort: M.
4. **Soap Suds / Beer Foam** ★★. Pour, watch the head rise, poke bubbles, watch the foam drain and pop.
   - Tech: 2D LBM free-surface with bubble tagging (HOME-FREE ideas) on WebGPU, plus thin-film colour on the bubble walls.
   - Effort: M–L.
5. **Snap the Branch** ★★. Bend twigs and branches until they crack. Green wood tears in long strands; dry wood snaps clean. Hook it into Core Haptics for the crack.
   - Tech: strand bundles of Cosserat rods with breakable links (Stressful Tree Modeling).
   - Effort: M.
6. **Bathtub** ★. Rubber ducks, boats and sponges bob and capsize. Pull the plug and watch the vortex drain.
   - Tech: Particles4All-style unified PBD on WebGPU, or reuse Splash Tank.
   - Effort: M.
7. **Clay Table** ★. Squish, pinch and roll plasticine blobs that keep their shape, and mix two colours by kneading.
   - Tech: 2.5D MLS-MPM with a plasticity yield and localised simulation around the finger (VR-Doh), plus the ISTA deformable texture for surface detail.
   - Effort: M.
8. **Erosion Table** ★. Sculpt a sand mound, then pour water: rivers carve, deltas form, and the sand slumps.
   - Tech: 2D height-field shallow water plus sediment transport (SIGGRAPH 2026 erosion honourable mention; Ripple Pool code).
   - Effort: M.
9. **Knot It** ★. Tie ropes into knots and pull them tight, with satisfying creak audio.
   - Tech: Stable Cosserat rods plus self-contact, with knot templates as "snap to knot" assists.
   - Effort: M–L.
10. **Bubble Wand** ★. Blow a soap bubble. Its iridescent film swirls as you move it, it wobbles and pops, and you can freeze it into frost.
    - Tech: thin-film interference colour on a sphere with a 2D surface vorticity sim (Viscous Vortex Dynamics on Surfaces), plus a freezing front.
    - Effort: M.
11. **Petri Dish**. Place food, and glowing slime mold grows vein networks to it. Physarum agents run on WebGPU.
    - Effort: S.
12. **Poke-a-Splat** (experimental). A captured 3DGS of a real object (a pudding, a plush Bobo) is deformed by a coarse shape-matching cage when you poke it. Rendered with Spark.
    - Budget: 100K to 200K splats **(est)**.
